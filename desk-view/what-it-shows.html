4471 · three date problems

owner Adam Burawski open reviewed 2026-11-12 · 0 days ago

Ticket #4471 merged every date report into one known issue and named a single fix: the parser reads the month first, and the tenants write the day first. The reports are separate problems. Each one has its own ticket. The counts below are computed from the export.

Parser

The shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant's dates come out flipped. This is every report that did not arrive through booking-v3's form, except the one-day moves.

Hint

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The form still reads the date the tenant's way, day first. People type the order the hint shows. This is a different bug from the parser, and it only arrives on the form channel.

One-day move

The desk read some reports as a shift of one day and listed their ids in desk-notes.json. They were filed under the parser cause because of the tenant. The calendar day moves, and the month and the day do not trade places. One of them is the same meeting shown on Tuesday in Lisbon and on Monday in Toronto.
